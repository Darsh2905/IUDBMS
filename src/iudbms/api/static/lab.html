The kernel, xHCI controller, HID and storage drivers, and the usbmon capture are real. The devices are QEMU's emulation, driven by recorded human keystroke timing. No physical BadUSB has been tested.

The models were trained on simulated traffic. Fast injection is caught mainly by the human speed rule, which flags a keyboard sending more reports per second than any measured typist. A payload typed once at human speed is not detected by anything here.
