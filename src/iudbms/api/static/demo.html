Quick start

Plug in a device to watch the real pipeline judge it.

Each card below is a live stream from the deployed models. The score climbs when a device stops behaving like a person. The alarm fires when 4 of its last 6 seconds are over the line, or when a keyboard types faster than any of 1,972 measured people.

This page streams the deployed Stage 1 and Stage 2 models, the calibrated threshold, the 4 of 6 gate, the model support list and the human speed rule. It uses the same detector code as the systemd monitor. Only the source of the USB traffic differs.

Simulated devices come from the generator the models were trained on, so their results show the pipeline working, not real world accuracy. Real kernel devices are recorded from emulated USB hardware on Debian 13, driven by replayed human input. No physical BadUSB has been tested.

Fast injection is caught mostly by the human speed rule. A payload typed once at human speed is not detected by anything here, and the demo shows that on purpose.
