Why detection isn't 100% across the board: mimicry_adaptive types its payload once and goes silent — no loop, no repeated pattern. Measured directly, zero of the 28 deployed behavioural features separate it from a human typing normally. That's a real architectural limit of watching one device in isolation, not a tuning problem.

The comparison that motivates the whole approach: a static VID:PID whitelist — the standard defence — detects 0% of every attack class above, because these attacks either spoof a trusted-looking identity or arrive on a device whose declared identity was never the problem. Removing the temporal model (Stage 2) and keeping only per-second scoring drops detection from 89.2% to 15.8%. Identity and single-second scoring both fall far short; watching behaviour over time is what closes the gap.
