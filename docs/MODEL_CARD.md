# IUDBMS model card

Generated by `python3 -m eval.model_card` from the trained artefacts and the code's own constants. Do not edit by hand.

Contract 3.0.0. Deployed model: `out/models`, trained on Corpus B; window threshold 0.994336 (target window FPR 0.0001); gate 4-of-6.

## Terminology

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| window | one device's traffic in one 1-second interval; the unit the models score |
| window-level recall | of post-onset attack windows, the fraction scored at or above the threshold |
| window-level false-positive rate | of benign windows, the fraction scored at or above the threshold (the calibration target, 1e-4) |
| session | one device from attachment to removal (one simulated device run) |
| session-level detection rate | of attack sessions, the fraction in which the 4-of-6 gate fires at least once after onset |
| session-level false-positive rate | of benign sessions, the fraction in which the gate fires at least once |
| false alarms per device-hour | gate firings on benign devices divided by the hours those devices were observed; the unit for long-running use |
| latency | time from attack onset to the end of the window that completed the gate |

## Normalisation

* **Stage 1:** per-feature z-score with the mean and SD of benign training windows; SD floored at 0.01 (SD_FLOOR) and the result clipped to +/-10.0 (STD_CLIP).
* **Stage 2:** per-input z-score with the mean and SD of all training sequence rows (SD clamped at 1e-6), stored in the checkpoint and applied in forward().

## Stage 1 (unsupervised, benign-only)

* **model:** Isolation Forest per declared class group (hid, storage, av, composite), plus a generic forest over all benign windows used for groups with fewer than 30 training windows ('other')
* **groups_fitted:** ["av", "hid", "other", "storage"]
* **n_estimators:** 200
* **contamination:** 0.02
* **max_samples:** min(512, windows in the group)
* **random_state:** 7
* **device_baseline:** {"type": "per-device EWMA of mean and variance", "alpha": 0.15, "warmup_windows": 4, "update_gate": 0.55, "variance_floor": 0.05}
* **fusion_weights:** {"forest": 0.55, "device_baseline": 0.45}
* **output:** anomaly score in [0, 1]; forest term scaled by its benign 99th percentile, device term by the benign 99th percentile of the deviation
* **trained_on:** benign windows of the fit split only (no labels)

## Stage 2 (supervised)

* **input:** the 28 deployed features + the Stage-1 score = 29 inputs per window, as sequences of the last 10 windows, left-padded by repeating the earliest row (strictly causal)
* **architecture:** 1-layer LSTM, hidden size 32, forget-gate bias 1; Linear(32 -> 1) on the last time step; sigmoid
* **output_layer:** one logit -> sigmoid = probability the current window is post-onset
* **loss:** binary cross-entropy with logits (BCEWithLogitsLoss)
* **class_weighting:** pos_weight = negative windows / positive windows in the training set (2.31 for Corpus B's fit split)
* **optimiser:** Adam, learning rate 0.002, weight decay 0.0, gradient norm clipped at 5.0
* **batch_size:** 128
* **epochs:** 14
* **stopping_rule:** fixed 14 epochs; no early stopping. Validation recall is logged per epoch but does not select the checkpoint
* **seed:** 7
* **labels:** window y = 1 iff the window ends after the attack onset

## Decision rule

* **window_threshold:** the (1 - target_fpr) quantile of Stage-2 scores on validation windows labelled benign (benign sessions and pre-onset windows of attack sessions)
* **gate:** alert when 4 of the last 6 windows of one device are at or above the threshold; the alert is timed at the end of the completing window
* **session_alarm:** a session counts as alarmed if the gate fires at least once
* **from_window_to_session:** not analytic: windows of one device are strongly autocorrelated (consecutive Stage-2 inputs share 9 of 10 rows), so session-level false alarms are MEASURED on held-out benign sessions and long sessions (out/analysis_session_fp.json)
* **enforcement:** THROTTLE then DEAUTHORIZE are generated, logged and stored but NOT applied: ENFORCEMENT_REVIEWED is False (dry-run)
